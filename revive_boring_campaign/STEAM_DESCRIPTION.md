[b][h1]Revive Boring Campaign[/h1][/b]

Bring dead campaigns back to life.

Kill, revive, boost, weaken, or designate a Nemesis rival among AI factions during a campaign through MCT.

Designed to revive dead campaigns, create chaos, save key factions, or shake up a world that has become too passive or predictable.

[hr][/hr]

[b]REQUIREMENTS[/b]

This mod requires:

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2927955021]
Mod Configuration Tool - v0.9 Beta
[/url]

The mod is controlled through MCT settings.

[b]FEATURES[/b]

[list]
[*]Kill an AI faction

[*]Anarchy Kill: destroy a faction and replace regions with rebels
(similar to Eshin mechanics)

[*]Revive dead factions

[list]
[*]Transfer starting province ownership
[*]Upgrade province capitals
[*]Grant 50,000 treasury
[*]Apply temporary free upkeep support
[/list]

[*]Boost AI factions

[list]
[*]Treasury support
[*]Additional armies
[*]Confederate specific faction or all subcultures factions
[*]Technologies
[*]Temporary upkeep support
[/list]

[*]Weaken AI factions

[list]
[*]Drain treasury
[*]Destroy armies
[/list]

[*]Nemesis: designate a persistent rival faction that the mod keeps alive and dangerous

[list]
[*]Immediate power boost on selection: revived if dead, all technologies unlocked, 50,000 gold, five armies
[*]Every 10 turns: revived if destroyed, boosted with gold and armies if outside the top 10 factions
[*]Every 25 turns: confederates the largest same-culture faction available
[*]Automatically declares war on the weakest hostile neighbor when at peace
[/list]

[/list]
